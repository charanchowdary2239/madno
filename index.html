ABOUT MADNO

Where Sweet Cravings Meet Unforgettable Memories

Madno House of Sundaes is a dessert-focused cafe designed especially for those who cherish sweet treats, joyful gatherings, and beautifully presented indulgences.

From towering artisanal sundaes and velvety thick shakes to crispy golden Belgian waffles, decadent brownies, and savory cafe bites, every creation at Madno is prepared with genuine passion, premium ingredients, and vibrant vibes for friends, families, and dessert lovers.
